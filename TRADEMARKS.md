name, logo, app icon, package identity, and publisher identity. It must remove

marketplace listing, and marketing material, except for a factual attribution
that does not imply an official release or endorsement.

If a distribution contains WorkTrail Brand Assets, obtain separate permission
or replace those assets before publishing it. The copyright and license notices
required by `LICENSE` must still be retained.
