# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and constructive.

Focus on technical substance, reproducible evidence, and actionable feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, deceptive submissions, and attempts to bypass project security or licensing controls are not acceptable.

## Enforcement and Reporting

Maintainers may remove content, close issues/PRs, or restrict participation when necessary.

For sensitive reports, use the private contact in `SECURITY.md` instead of a public issue.
