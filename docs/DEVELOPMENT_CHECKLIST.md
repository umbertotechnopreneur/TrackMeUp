Aurora annotations are approximate eligibility indicators, not visibility probabilities. They use WMM2025 centered-dipole magnetic latitude (including longitude), the approximate Kp-dependent oval edge with a horizon-view allowance, and solar altitude below −6°. Forecast darkness is sampled every 30 minutes. Clouds, terrain and moonlight can still prevent observation. See [NOAA viewing guidance](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/content/tips-viewing-aurora) and [NCEI geomagnetic poles](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/wandering-geomagnetic-poles). Alerts without an explicit expiry are limited to 24 hours after issue; unavailable optional NOAA feeds produce no annotation.
