# First-party AI asset provenance

## Project owner declaration

On 2026-08-30, project owner Umberto Giacobbi confirmed that the current
first-party TrackMeUp visual assets listed below were created with AI-assisted
image-generation workflows run on systems under his control. He selected and
reviewed the resulting artwork and authorized its inclusion in the public
TrackMeUp source repository and official TrackMeUp binary distributions.

This declaration records the source category, production method, human review,
and intended publication scope based on the project owner's first-hand account.
Exact historical model names, versions, and prompts were not retained for every
older asset. Newer asset directories preserve more detailed prompts, manifests,
and deterministic processing records where available.

## Covered assets

The declaration covers these first-party asset families as they exist on the
declaration date:

- `trackmeup-icon-reference.png` and the application identity assets generated
  from it under `TrackMeUp/Assets/`;
- the Atomic Nuke privacy banners under `atomic-nuke/output/`;
- the Recall Timeline source and generated outputs under `recall-timeline/`;
- `TrackMeUp/Assets/TrackMeUpSnapshotPlaceholder.png`;
- `TrackMeUp/Assets/Celestial/sun-premium.png` and
  `TrackMeUp/Assets/Celestial/moon-premium.png`; and
- the text-prompt-generated world-clock watercolor pilot recorded under
  `design/world-clocks/watercolor/pilot-v1/`;
- the manifest-defined selected Urban Wash city masters and their deterministic WebP
  derivatives under `design/world-clocks/watercolor/` and
  `TrackMeUp/Assets/WorldClocks/Skylines/`; and
- the 11 composable atmosphere overlays under
  `TrackMeUp/Assets/WorldClocks/Overlays/`.

The icon, Atomic Nuke, and Recall Timeline directories retain their more
specific provenance records. Generated derivatives remain traceable through
their repository scripts and manifests.

## License and third-party boundary

TrackMeUp identity assets and branded marketing artwork remain TrackMeUp Brand
Assets governed by the repository's
[Trademark and Brand Policy](../../TRADEMARKS.md); this declaration does not
relicense them under MIT or grant trademark rights.

This declaration does not cover third-party data, software, fonts, or media.
In particular, it does not replace the GeoNames record for the packaged
world-clock catalog. The manifest-defined Urban Wash catalog and atmosphere overlays
retain their separate generation manifests, checksums, and asset-specific
license boundary. Any asset derived from third-party material must keep its
original source, license, attribution, and applicable redistribution
obligations regardless of later AI-assisted or deterministic processing.

On 2026-08-30 the project owner explicitly authorized public publication of the
generated Urban Wash city artwork and atmosphere overlays and confirmed the
applicable ImageGen publication scope. That authorization does not relicense
the artwork under MIT.

This provenance record is not a legal conclusion about copyrightability or
third-party rights. It records the project owner's generation and publication
declaration for release review.
