# Atomic Nuke banner provenance

This record accompanies these TrackMeUp privacy banners:

- `output/trackmeup-atomic-privacy-banner-v2-dark-erasure-wave-2400x800.png`;
- `output/trackmeup-atomic-privacy-banner-v3-light-radial-reset-2400x800.png`.

Repository history first records both files in commit `b91f3d7` on 2026-08-10
(`docs: add atomic privacy banners`).

On 2026-08-30, project owner Umberto Giacobbi confirmed that both banners were
created with AI-assisted image-generation workflows run on systems under his
control. The exact historical model, version, and prompts were not retained.
He selected and reviewed both theme variants and authorized them for
publication in the public TrackMeUp source repository and official TrackMeUp
binaries. The repository-wide declaration is recorded in
[`../AI_ASSET_PROVENANCE.md`](../AI_ASSET_PROVENANCE.md).
